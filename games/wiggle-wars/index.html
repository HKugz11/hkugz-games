How to play
Mouse steer: your worm follows the pointer
Click & hold or Space / Shift boost (uses a little length and leaves a trail of dots)
A / D or Arrows steer left and right
Phone / tablet drag on the screen to steer, hold the BOOST button
Tip: cut in front of a worm so it bumps into you
Tip: when a worm bumps and pops, it drops lots of dots. Grab them!
Tip: the edge of the arena is soft. It just slides you along
P or Esc pause
