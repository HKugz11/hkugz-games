ESCAPE
DA TRAZ

Sneak through five wings of the prison. Pick each lock, freeze when a guard's searchlight sweeps your way, then make a run for the fence.

Controls
WASD / arrows: sneak around
Drag mouse (or the right side on touch) to look around
E or Space: start picking a lock, then hit it again in the green zone
Esc: pause
Tip: standing still is much safer than walking when a searchlight is near
Tip: dark patches on the floor help hide you too
Tip: getting caught just resets you to the start of that wing
Tip: missing a lock pick makes noise - guards notice faster
Settings
