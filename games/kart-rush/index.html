RUSH

Drift, grab item boxes, and beat seven robots. No blood, just confetti.

Controls
W / Up gas   S / Down brake and reverse
A D / Left Right steer
Shift or Space drift: hold it in a turn, steer through it, then let go for a mini-turbo (blue, orange, purple = bigger)
E, Enter or click use your item
R put yourself back on the road   Esc pause
Rocket start: press gas just as the countdown hits GO
Item boxes give you turbo, rockets, oil slicks, a shield or a zap. The further back you are, the better the items!
Phones and tablets: left thumb steers, DRIFT is the big button, gas is automatic
