Controls
WASD move   Space jump   Shift crouch
Mouse aim   Click shoot / place   Right click zoom
1-5 pickaxe, AR, shotgun, SMG, sniper   Wheel switch
Q build mode   Z wall   X floor   C stairs   V roof
G edit the wall or floor you look at: click tiles to cut them, 1-4 quick shapes (door, window...), G again to confirm
B wood / stone / metal   R reload, or rotate stairs
T toggle zoom (good on a trackpad)   F first / third person   Tab scoreboard   Esc pause
Pickaxe hits trees, rocks, crates and builds to gather materials
Range: U reset builds, K sparring bot
Tip: jump and place a floor under you, then stairs, to ramp up
Settings

This game needs a keyboard and mouse. Open it on a computer!

Online play connects you straight to your friends through a free helper service (PeerJS). Only share your room code with people you know, because players can see each other's network address.
