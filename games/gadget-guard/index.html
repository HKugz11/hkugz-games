Build gadgets, stop the robots, protect the core. No blood, just confetti.

How to play
Tap or click a square next to the road, then pick a gadget to build.
Tap a gadget to upgrade it (4 levels), sell it, or change who it shoots first.
Pop Blaster is fast and cheap. Boomer splashes crowds but cannot hit flyers. Frost Zapper slows robots. Rail Sniper pierces armor from far away. Tesla Coil chains lightning. Booster powers up its neighbours.
Armor makes weak shots almost useless: Tanks and MEGA BOTs need heavy hitters.
Space next wave   F speed   A airstrike   1-6 build   U upgrade   S sell   T target   Esc pause
Drag to move the view, wheel or pinch to zoom. Calling a wave early pays a bonus!
