Tips: stop moving before you shoot (moving = shots fly everywhere). Tap or burst at long range; pull your mouse down while spraying. Headshots do 4x. Hold right-click (or press Z) to aim: the green crosshair pulls in tight and you zoom in a little (or switch to iron sights in Settings). Snipers use right-click for the full scope. Shift sprints, but you cannot shoot while sprinting. Running is loud: enemies hear your footsteps, X-walking is silent.
