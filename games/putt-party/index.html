PUTT
PARTY

Toon mini golf with windmills, bumpers, sand and sneaky pipes. Everybody putts at the same time!

How to play
Mouse or finger: press, drag back away from your ball (like a slingshot), aim the arrow and let go. The further you pull, the harder the putt.
Keyboard: left and right arrows (or A / D) turn the arrow, hold Space to charge and let go to putt.
The dotted line shows where the ball goes until it hits something. Walls bounce it, bumpers boing it, sand slows it down.
Land in water and you get one extra stroke. A ball that is too fast rolls right over the cup!
Mouse wheel zooms   Esc pauses   Eight strokes is the limit on a hole.
The lowest total strokes after the last hole wins.
