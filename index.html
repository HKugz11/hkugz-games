Play together

Invite your friends to a game

Make a free account (just a username and a password, no email), add your friends, and invite them to a room in Blaster Arena, Fort Fight or Core Strike. They see the invite right on the page and press Join. There is no chat, only game invites.

What is stored

You need a sign-up code from HKugz to make an account.

  1. Make an accountA username and a password, nothing else. Never use your real name.
  2. Add friendsBy username or friend code. They accept, and you can see who is online.
  3. Host and inviteStart a game, press Host, then invite a friend who is online. They press Join or Enter.

For game makers

Made a game? Put it here!

This is a great place to start. If you make games and want more people to play yours, send it to me. I usually put games up here with your name on them, and I am reaching out to other young developers too. Anything that runs in a web page works: HTML, Scratch, Unity WebGL, and more.

I play every game before I put it up, I always credit the person who made it, and if you ever want yours taken down, just ask.
